Service Quote Lab · Research pilot v0.4

Cleaner quotes, without the guesswork.

Build a room-by-room cleaning estimate, account for your real costs and see a suggested quote. No account or tracking. Your entries stay in this browser. You can use it offline after downloading the page.

1. Rooms and estimated cleaning time

Start with example times and adjust for your own experience. These durations are fictional demonstration defaults, not published industry standards.

Room time = your base minutes × chosen condition multiplier. Extras such as oven cleaning can be added as another line.

2. Costs and business assumptions

Enter real hourly labour cost, travel, consumables and allocated overheads. A margin is different from a markup.

Labour cost can include the value of your own time, not just wages. Enter VAT only if appropriate for your business; this demo does not determine VAT registration or tax treatment.
